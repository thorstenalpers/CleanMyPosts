### What's Changed

This release gives the updater a face. Until now "Check for updates" was the last thing the
app said before it disappeared and came back a different version.

**Updating**

* New: An update is offered before it is installed. The dialog names the version and says
  plainly that the app closes and reopens, so a running deletion is never cut short by a
  restart nobody asked for.
* New: The download reports its progress. Previously the button simply sat there for however
  long the installer took, with nothing to distinguish a slow connection from a hung app.
* Fix: "No updates available." arrived in English regardless of the selected language — it
  was written into the host rather than the catalogue. Every message the updater shows is
  now translated.
